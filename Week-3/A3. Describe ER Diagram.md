This is a University Database Design showing how different entities (people and things) are connected. It has four main tables:

#Student	

    Existing: Name, Code, NID, Dates	

    Missing: Student_ID (PK), Timestamps

#Lecturer	

    Existing: Name, Email, Address	

    Missing: Lecturer_ID (PK), Department, Timestamps

#Subject	

    Existing: Code, Name, Units	

    Missing: Subject_ID (PK), Description, Level, Timestamps

#Enrollment	

    Existing: Time, CC#	

    Missing: Enrollment_ID (PK), FK References, Grade, Status, Semester

#Lecture	
    Existing: Date, CC#	
    Missing: Lecture_ID (PK), FK References, Room, Time, Day, Timestamps

The arrows show relationships:

#Student → Enrollment (One student can enroll in many subjects)

#Subject → Enrollment (One subject can have many students)

#Lecture → Subject (One subject can have many lectures, or one lecturer teaches many subjects)

The main missing elements are primary keys (IDs) for most tables, proper data types, validation rules, and relationship constraints
